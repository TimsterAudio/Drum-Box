## 0.1.0 (2026-10-10)

First version.
- An 808 kick section: Tune, Decay, Sweep, Snap, Click, Drive, Tone, FX send, Level, Mute, Solo, Choke, a play pad and a live waveform drawing.
- Ten more 808-style instruments (snare, clap, rim shot, three toms, cowbell, closed and open hats, cymbal), each with Tune, Decay, Tone, Pan, FX send, Level, Mute, Solo and a pad. The closed hat chokes the open hat.
- A 16-step grid with an Accent row; click or drag to place hits.
- Patterns A–H with queued switching, Length, Accent, Swing, Tempo, Chain, Rec, Copy, Paste, Clear and Random.
- Bus Drive and Glue, a tempo-synced delay and a reverb, master volume, a safety limiter, meters, a scope and a spectrum.
- Factory kits: Trap 808, Boom Bap, Electro Funk, House, Miami Bass and Init. Save, Delete, Export and Import.
- The Drone Box panel: Black or Silver, Colour or Pointer knobs, and fit-to-window scaling.
- `tests/smoke.js`, a headless Chromium smoke test.
